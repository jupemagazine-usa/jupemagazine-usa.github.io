Sweet Vanilla Records

The Label of Aaniyah Anderson — New Orleans

A record label holds its name at this ground. Sweet Vanilla Records — the label of Aaniyah Anderson, its founder and chief executive — delivers its records through the house's white-label machinery via Symphonic, under her name.

01

The label

Sweet Vanilla Records is the record label of Aaniyah Anderson. She founded it, and she runs it — its founder and chief executive. Her catalog and her rights stay hers.

Byron Jupiter is her mentor, and he administers her intellectual property. The house's label serves as the white-label vehicle for hers: delivery runs through Symphonic under her label's name — the machinery behind her brand, never in place of it.

02

The seal

A pink orchid over a gold record, green laurels at either side, the name on a burgundy ribbon — the seal of the label, whole and in color. It opens and closes the films the house builds for the label.

03

The releases
