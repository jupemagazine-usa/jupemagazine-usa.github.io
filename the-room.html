The Room

Tracking · Mixing · Archive Delivery

One recording room, kept to a signal standard: treated, itemized, and documented session by session.

01

What the room does

Recording & production — Tracking, overdubs, mixing, and archive-grade delivery for music, spoken word, and documentary work.

Video, cameras & streaming — Mirrorless and DSLR systems, livestream capture, lighting interfaces, and secure media storage for interviews, documentaries, commercials, and training.

02

Equipment on the record

The room's equipment is kept as a written record, and the items used are itemized per session. Held apart from the working chain stands the Neve V3 — retired, a Late 1900s museum artifact, held for historical research and development.

The working chain, selected from the record — Neve 1073SPX-D preamp and EQ · Avalon VT-737sp tube channel strip · Manley CORE reference channel strip · SSL Nucleus² and SSL 2 control · Universal Audio Apollo interface · RME MADIface USB · Dangerous Music D-BOX monitor control and summing · Genelec 1032A monitors · Meyer Sound monitoring — HD-1, and the 833 and 834 set with subwoofer.

Microphones on the record — Manley Reference Cardioid · Peluso 22 47 SE tube microphone · Sony C-80.

03

The signal standard

Practical discipline in gain staging, signal flow, cable care, testing, soldering, shielding, labeling, storage, and maintenance logs protects production quality — reducing noise, grounding problems, signal loss, and cable failure.

The result is self-reliance and equipment longevity, serving the studio, the stage, the archive, and the classroom at once.
