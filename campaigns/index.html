The Campaign Desk

Creator campaigns for the records — researched, briefed, booked, and checked in-house. Every move approved. Payouts tracked, never sent.

The law of this desk. It never invents a creator, a rate, or a contact — every name on the board is a real one the house supplied. It drafts; it does not send. Silence is never an approval. Money is committed when a creator says yes, not when a draft goes out. Paid social ads are not this desk's work.

2 · The Brief

Built from the sound notes above. A real camera-roll moment beats a staged one — no default dance challenge, no celebrity chase. The brief does not exist until it is approved, and no outreach draft is written before that approval.

3 · The Creators

Add real creators only — from those already using the sound, then the ones like them. Ranked by the median views of their recent posts, not the follower count. The list is built at about 2.5× what the budget can cover.

4 · The Deal Board

One approval sends the batch: each creator marked Contacted gets an outreach draft under 120 words, written on behalf of the artist, shown before anything leaves the house. No reply gets one nudge, then the thread closes. Above-band asks, exclusivity, and usage changes stop at Byron's desk.




5 · The Checks


When a post lands, mark it Posted on the board with its link. This desk then watches it on days 3, 7, 14, and 30: still up, public, carrying the sound. A pulled post or a swapped sound flags the creator off future shortlists.
