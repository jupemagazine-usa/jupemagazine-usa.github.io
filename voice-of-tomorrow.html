The Voice of Tomorrow

In Session — In Residence at the Ground — A Micro-Documentary Series

A film series holds residence at this ground. The Voice of Tomorrow — In Session, the micro-documentary series by the Byron Jupiter Agency for The Box Studios, makes its home at 7367 Chef Menteur Hwy — its sessions held and its films made at the museum ground, under its own name.

01

The residency at the ground

The Voice of Tomorrow — In Session is in residence at The Box Studios, 7367 Chef Menteur Hwy, New Orleans, LA 70126. The residency seats the series at the museum ground: its sessions are held here, its filming is staged here, and its episodes are seated on the house's own doors.

The series and the museum stay distinct. The Box Studios is a museum of music and media with a recording studio inside it. The Voice of Tomorrow is its film series — a production of the Byron Jupiter Agency for The Box Studios — and it carries its own name, its own episodes, and its own door.

02

The series

In its own words: “A micro-documentary series: one artist, one session, one discipline.” Each episode takes a single artist into a single session and stays with the work — the discipline of it, in the room where it happens.

Episodes run short — three to five minutes — and arrive monthly. Full episodes are seated on the house's own surfaces; short teasers travel the open doors and point home.

03

The pilot

The first episode is made: “In Session: Aaniyah. at the Dew Drop Inn” — the pilot artist Aaniyah Anderson, filmed from her session at the Dew Drop Inn, New Orleans.

The episode's seating is announced on the series' own door as it opens. The series' standing collection — performances gathered under its name — stands open on that door today.

04

Sessions under its name

The residency runs both ways. Recording sessions at The Box are held under the series' sponsorship — a recording session sponsored by Voice of Tomorrow, at the Bucktown Attic Studios, the recording studio inside The Box Studios.

The session form is the house's standing form — one artist, one session, one discipline — whether the camera is rolling or the record light is on.

05

Its own door

The series keeps its own door: its standing collection stands open on YouTube, under its own name — The Voice of Tomorrow — In Session.

Questions about the series come to The Box Studios, through its two doors. Questions about the ground itself come the same way.

Watch the series — The Voice of Tomorrow — In Session — the series' own collection on YouTube: its performances and episodes under one name.

06

The night at Gasa Gasa

On November 22, 2026, the series was in the room at Gasa Gasa, 4920 Freret St, New Orleans, where Aaniyah Anderson headlined Spinning Soul, presented by Howl Entertainment. The camera stayed with her — at the microphone, in the neon, and in a quiet moment off stage.

Byron Jupiter Agency, documenting The Voice of Tomorrow — Aaniyah Anderson.
