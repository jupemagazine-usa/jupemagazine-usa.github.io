Before the Show

Aaniyah Anderson — Gasa Gasa, New Orleans — November 22, 2026

A short document of the room before the music. Before the Show holds the moments ahead of Aaniyah Anderson's performance at Gasa Gasa on November 22, 2026 — a Voice of Tomorrow document, bookended by the seal of Sweet Vanilla Records.

01

The piece

November 22, 2026. Aaniyah Anderson, in the room before her show at Gasa Gasa, 4920 Freret Street, New Orleans — presented by Howl Entertainment. The smile, the walk to the bar, the hug: the minutes before the performance, as they happened.

A Voice of Tomorrow document, built by the house. The full performance film from the same night is in The Voice of Tomorrow — In Session.
