The Ground

Building · Garden Lot · The Sign

The studio stands on its own ground: a commercial building on Chef Menteur Highway in New Orleans, owned and held, with a garden lot beside it.

01

The building

The building at 7367 Chef Menteur Hwy houses The Box Studios — the recording room, the archive's working space, and the studio's offices under one roof. Portions of the building are suited to lease to compatible tenants.

A permitted pole sign serves the premises. The building itself carries the studio's name on the highway.

02

The garden lot

Beside the building, the garden lot carries the Tranquility Garden of Serenity and the house's exhibition ground — a place held for gatherings, teaching days, and the archive's public face.

A model home is planned for the back corner of the garden lot, set facing the oak and away from the street.

03

The foundation

The ground earns independently of the studio's calendar, and it is the foundation every other lane stands on.

Leasing inquiries and ground questions route to the studio's doors: sales@leasethebox.com.
