The Box Studios

Publishing · Compositions & Phonograms · Books · Apps · Films

A museum of music and media in the Eastern New Orleans Renaissance Corridor — one room where the work is made, and a system around it that publishes it, licenses it, and keeps it: compositions and phonograms, books and their tools, apps, and films, all carried in one registry and reached through two doors only.

01

The museum

The Box Studios is a museum with a room at its center. The room is a working recording studio — tracking, overdubs, mixing, and archive-grade delivery for music, spoken word, and documentary work — and what is made in it feeds the whole museum: publishing administration, compositions and phonograms for license, books and their tools, apps, and films. The room is the origin. The system is the business.

The engines, in plain words. Publishing: Good Delivery administers publishing — the museum's own catalog, and other creators' catalogs white-label, under their own names; the artists keep their rights, and the museum never takes ownership. Licensing: compositions and phonograms are licensed by tier — Lease, Premium, Unlimited, Exclusive — and quoted by scope; a buyer receives a use, never the original. Books: a museum book opens its own website and the tools built around it; the book is a door, not the whole product. Apps: one price — $9.99, paid once; no subscription. Films: produced and released by the museum.

One roof, owned ground, one ownership: the room, the publishing desk, the presses, and the archive — and every lane feeds the one registry. The museum registry holds 163 (registry as of 2026-10-25) registered works to date — the registered fraction of a larger body of work, administered from this room. (registry as of 2026-10-25)

Two doors

The museum keeps two doors, and only two. Email: sales@leasethebox.com. And Enter The B · O · X — the chat, where matters are opened and answered in writing. No phone, no form, no other door.

Both doors are kept by the desk. The desk is Museum — the agent of the Byron Jupiter Agency — who answers from the record, carries each matter to Byron Jupiter, and brings the answer back in writing. Byron Jupiter is reached through the desk, and only through the desk. There is no direct line to him, and no other way in.
